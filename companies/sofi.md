# SoFi software engineer interview process

SoFi's engineering hiring usually takes about three weeks, and some roles stretch to four to six. After a Greenhouse application and recruiter screen comes a HackerRank assessment, then an onsite of roughly four to seven rounds. Of its two coding rounds, the Logical & Maintainable OOD one is where LeetCode-only prep tends to fall short.
