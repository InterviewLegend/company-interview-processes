# MongoDB software engineer interview process

MongoDB's interview process usually takes three to five weeks: a recruiter call, one or two tech screens, a hiring manager talk, then a virtual onsite. Its three to four 60-minute Zoom rounds cover coding, a component build, database-internals design and behavioral. Coding often gains a thread-safe follow-up, and the team decides in a debrief.
