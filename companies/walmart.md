# Walmart software engineer interview process

Walmart's software loop runs a recruiter screen, a front-door filter, then three to five one-hour rounds in a virtual onsite. In the US that filter is usually a 60-minute Karat screen, while India and campus hires take a test on HackerRank or HirePro. Four to eight weeks is common. From Senior up, design decides it.
