# Rubrik software engineer interview process

Rubrik's experienced-hire process takes a few weeks to two months: a recruiter call, one or two CoderPad screens, then usually four to six onsite rounds. Commonly two or three are System Coding, where concurrent code must compile and run. New grads first sit a proctored HackerRank test, four problems in roughly 75 to 90 minutes.
