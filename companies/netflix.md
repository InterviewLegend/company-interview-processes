# Netflix software engineer interview process

Most Netflix engineering loops take three to five weeks and four stages, ending in a virtual onsite with four or five rounds. Before it come a recruiter call, a screen with your future manager and a CoderPad or CodeSignal phone screen. Design and culture weigh most, and the manager decides with no hiring committee.

|---|---|
| Stages | 4 stages |
| Timeline | ~3–5 weeks |
