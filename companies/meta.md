# Meta software engineer interview process

Meta's SWE process runs five stages, usually over a few weeks to two months: recruiter screen, phone screen, full loop, team match and offer. The 45-minute CoderPad phone screen has two problems. A hiring committee decides after a loop of four or more rounds, and most offers then wait on a team match.
