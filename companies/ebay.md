# eBay software engineer interview process

eBay's loop usually runs a recruiter call, a CodeSignal test and a phone screen, then four or five onsite rounds of 45 to 60 minutes. Coding fills two or three, plus system design and a manager round. US verdicts usually take a week. In India it's one Bengaluru weekend, and silence is the top complaint.
