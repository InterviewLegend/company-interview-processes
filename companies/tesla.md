# Tesla software engineer interview process

Tesla's software engineer loop usually runs five stages and averages about 33 days, anywhere from two weeks to two months. A recruiter screen and an early hiring manager call lead to an hour of live coding on CoderPad or CodeSignal, then a three to five round onsite. A vague "why Tesla" gets people rejected.
