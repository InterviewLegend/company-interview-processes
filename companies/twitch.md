# Twitch software engineer interview process

Getting a Twitch offer usually takes two to eight weeks, through Amazon's hiring machine under Twitch's own stage names. After a recruiter chat and a manager meeting come the async Side Quest test and a phone screen. The Final Boss onsite is four to five rounds, with Leadership Principles graded in nearly all of them.
