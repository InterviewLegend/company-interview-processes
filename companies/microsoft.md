# Microsoft software engineer interview process

Microsoft's process usually takes a few weeks: a recruiter screen, an online assessment some orgs skip, then one day of four or five 45-minute interviews. Each opens with 10 to 15 minutes of behavioral. The final, as-appropriate slot often belongs to the hiring manager or a senior engineer. Decisions take days to two weeks.
