# Notion software engineer interview process

Notion's process usually takes two to five weeks: recruiter call, 60-minute CoderPad screen, a virtual onsite of four or five rounds, then a leadership final. The screen is the elimination gate. Onsite brings coding, the round Notion calls High-Level Design and Data Modeling, and a values behavioral, with collaboration design added from mid level up.

|---|---|
| Stages | ~4–5 onsite rounds |
| Timeline | ~2–5 weeks |
