# Pinterest software engineer interview process

Pinterest's process usually takes three to five weeks from recruiter call to offer. A recruiter screen and a technical phone screen lead to about five virtual onsite rounds. For a generalist SWE that's three coding, one system design and a hiring-manager behavioral. Even then, no offer comes until a team picks you up.
