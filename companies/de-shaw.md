# D. E. Shaw software engineer interview process

D. E. Shaw hires developers in six stages, from a HackerRank test and a CodePair live round to three to five onsite tech rounds. A techno-managerial talk and HR follow. Each test problem has its own timer, and most people are cut there. India onsites often fit in one day.
