# Datadog software engineer interview process

Datadog's loop usually runs three to six weeks: a half-hour recruiter call, an hour of CoderPad, then a virtual onsite of four or five rounds. The onsite pairs two coding rounds with one design and one values round, sometimes plus a deep-dive. Most rejections come in design, and team matching follows a pass.
