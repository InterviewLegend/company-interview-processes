# Wells Fargo software engineer interview process

Expect two to six weeks and three or four stages in the Wells Fargo software engineer loop. A recruiter screen and an online assessment lead to one or two 45-minute technical panels, then a technical-managerial final with the hiring manager. Most people fall at the assessment, a 70 to 90 minute timed test.
