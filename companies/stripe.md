# Stripe software engineer interview process

Stripe's loop usually takes four to eight weeks, nearer two with a referral. A roughly 30-minute recruiter call and a live coding screen lead to an onsite of four or five practical rounds, most 45 to 60 minutes. Bug squash, widely called the hardest to prep for, by reports weighs heavily in the debrief.
