# Wayfair software engineer interview process

Run in full by its Bengaluru org, Wayfair's loop opens with a recruiter screen and a HackerRank or Karat coding test. A coding round follows, often with a coupon problem on a category tree, then two design rounds and behavioral. US loops are shorter. Design and the coupon round usually decide it.
