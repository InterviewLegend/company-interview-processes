# NVIDIA software engineer interview process

From first call to offer, NVIDIA's hiring usually takes three to eight weeks. A recruiter screen and one or more phone screens of 45 to 60 minutes lead to an onsite of about five rounds. Many portal applicants first take a HackerRank or Codility test. Your future team builds the loop and decides.

| | |
|---|---|

| Timeline | ~3–8 weeks |
| Online assessment | HackerRank or Codility |
