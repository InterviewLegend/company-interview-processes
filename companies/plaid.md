# Plaid software engineer interview process

Plaid's engineering loop usually runs two and a half to three weeks and ends in a virtual onsite with four or five rounds. Before it come a recruiter call, sometimes a 90-minute online assessment, and a CoderPad-style live coding screen. The onsite mixes practical coding, a bank-data aggregation design round, a project deep-dive and behavioral.
