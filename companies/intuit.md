# Intuit software engineer interview process

Intuit's loop often takes two to four weeks: recruiter screen, Glider assessment, a phone screen called the "Eliminator", then the A4A onsite. The onsite's roughly four rounds hinge on the Craft Demo, where you build on a real codebase or present a take-home. That round decides most offers, and a newer AI round sometimes joins.
