# American Express software engineer interview process

American Express's India loop for experienced hires usually runs three rounds: technical, techno-managerial, then an HR or salary talk. Campus hires take a timed online test of about 90 minutes first. Coding sits near LeetCode medium, but people who prepped only that tend to come up short on Java and Spring internals.
