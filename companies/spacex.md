# SpaceX software engineer interview process

SpaceX's software hiring usually takes five to seven stages: a recruiter call, a take-home of roughly four to six hours, and tech screens. Then comes an onsite of about five hours, with four or five coding and design rounds, where the project talk you defend to a panel matters more than the coding.
