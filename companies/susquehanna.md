# Susquehanna software engineer interview process

Susquehanna's software loop usually runs two to four weeks, from recruiter screen to a Super Day of three or four back-to-back sessions. In between sit a CodeSignal test, about four questions in 70 minutes, and one or two live rounds. That test cuts most people, with a reported pass rate of 20 to 30 percent.
