# Applied Intuition software engineer interview process

Applied Intuition's loop usually takes two to four weeks: a recruiter or hiring-manager screen, a coding screen with no AI, then an onsite super day. That day runs roughly four to six rounds back to back, and pass-rate estimates sit near single digits. Interviewers lean on C++ and object-oriented design more than web-app fluency.
