# Bloomberg software engineer interview process

Bloomberg's engineering loop runs four to seven weeks for most people: a recruiter call, one or two hour-long HackerRank CodePair screens, then a superday. Nearly every technical round packs in two problems, and anyone past entry level also gets system design. People often get cut for sinking the whole hour into problem one.
