# Discord software engineer interview process

Discord's interview runs four stages over about three to five weeks: recruiter screen, skills test, virtual onsite and a hiring-manager debrief. Onsite you face four or five rounds of roughly 45 minutes, coding on your own laptop with docs open. Two behavioral rounds, Values and Attitude, can sink a strong coder.
