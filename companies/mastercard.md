# Mastercard software engineer interview process

Mastercard's India loop commonly runs four to five touchpoints, built around a three-round Superday: a Technical SME, a Bar Raiser and a Hiring Manager round. Before it comes a roughly 45-minute knockout screen; campus hires also take a two-question, hour-long online test. Rounds tend to turn on Java fluency more than on the algorithms.
