# Lyft software engineer interview process

Lyft's loop is a recruiter call, a one-hour CoderPad screen and a four-round virtual onsite, with a decision usually in one to two weeks. The onsite turns on the Laptop test, building a runnable program in your own editor, alongside one or two design rounds and a manager talk. Team matching follows the verbal offer.
