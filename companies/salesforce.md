# Salesforce software engineer interview process

Salesforce's software loop usually takes three to six weeks across six stages. A recruiter call leads to a HackerRank test of about 90 minutes, heavy on dynamic programming and widely called the hardest gate. A phone screen and a virtual onsite of about four rounds follow, then a hiring-manager decision and team match.

|---|---|

| Timeline | ~3–6 weeks |
