# Hudson River Trading software engineer interview process

Hudson River Trading's Core loop runs four stages: application, a timed coding test, roughly two phone screens and a full final day. The test is about four problems, usually on CodeSignal, graded on hidden cases and speed. The final day adds a debugging round in code you didn't write. Probability puzzles gate the research tracks.
