# OpenAI software engineer interview process

OpenAI's process commonly runs six to eight weeks, from a recruiter screen through one or two CoderPad phone screens to a virtual onsite. The onsite holds four to six rounds, usually two coding, system design, behavioral and a project deep-dive you present. Team matching and leveling follow, often landing a rung below FAANG.
