# Google software engineer interview process

Google's software engineer process has seven stages and usually takes six to twelve weeks. A pass/fail phone screen, often in a Google Doc, leads to an onsite of four or five 45-minute rounds. Then a hiring committee that never met you decides, and team matching can run from a week to several months.

| Stages | 7 stages |
| Timeline | ~6–12 weeks |
