# Instacart software engineer interview process

Instacart's hiring process commonly runs four to six weeks. A recruiter screen leads to a roughly 70-minute CodeSignal test with four tasks, then a phone screen and four or five virtual onsite rounds. One debrief decides both hire and level, and design is where senior candidates most often get pushed down a rung.
