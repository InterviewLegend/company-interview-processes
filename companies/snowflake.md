# Snowflake software engineer interview process

Snowflake's process commonly runs three to six weeks, from a recruiter call and an online test or phone screen to a virtual onsite. The onsite runs four or five hour-long rounds over Zoom, plus a Tech Talk from IC3 up. Coding and system design trip people most, then the team decides in a debrief.
