# Anthropic software engineer interview process

Anthropic's engineering loop usually runs three to four weeks over five or six stages. A recruiter screen and a 90-minute CodeSignal test or a 60-minute live screen come first, sometimes with a hiring-manager chat, before a four- or five-round virtual onsite. By most accounts, the standalone Values round cuts more strong engineers than any other.
