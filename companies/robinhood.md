# Robinhood software engineer interview process

Robinhood's process usually runs five stages over four to six weeks. A recruiter call leads to a Karat or CodeSignal screen, then a virtual Super Day of three to five rounds on CoderPad. A central debrief sets hire and level before team matching. Coding is graded on correctness, with a "now make it thread-safe" follow-up.

| | |
|---|---|
| Stages | 3–5 onsite rounds |
| Timeline | ~4–6 weeks |
