# Roblox software engineer interview process

Roblox's software engineer loop runs four stages and commonly takes four to eight weeks. A recruiter screen and a CodeSignal test or tech screen lead to three to five onsite rounds, including the graded creativity round, then a final values round. A hiring committee sets your level and can overrule even a team's favorite.

| | |
|---|---|
| Stages | 4 stages |
| Timeline | ~4–8 weeks |
| Online assessment | CodeSignal |
