# Perplexity software engineer interview process

A Perplexity software engineer loop usually takes one to three weeks across four to six stages. A recruiter screen and one or two applied Python phone screens lead to an onsite of four or five virtual rounds, then a founder or leader talk. The design round often asks for a stripped-down Perplexity.

| | |
|---|---|
| Stages | ~4–6 stages |
| Timeline | ~1–3 weeks |
