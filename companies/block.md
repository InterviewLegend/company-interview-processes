# Block software engineer interview process

At Block, the interview process commonly takes about six weeks: a recruiter screen, a CoderPad phone screen, then a virtual onsite called the loop. Its four to five scored rounds run about 4.5 hours, usually two coding, one design, behavioral and a past project. Down-levels mostly trace to thin design and scope signal.
