# IBM software engineer interview process

IBM's software loop usually has three to five stages, with most coding in a HackerRank test of about two easy-to-medium problems. A recruiter screen comes first, then one or two live rounds and a behavioral or HR close. It commonly takes about six weeks, and most of the delay comes from a multi-layer offer sign-off.

| | |
|---|---|
| Stages | up to 5 stages |
| Timeline | ~6 weeks |
| Online assessment | HackerRank |
