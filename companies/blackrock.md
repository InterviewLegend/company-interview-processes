# BlackRock software engineer interview process

BlackRock's software engineer interview usually runs three rounds after a screen, over three to four weeks, and it splits by region. In India it's often a one-day hiring drive: coding on HackerRank CodePair, a Java fundamentals deep-dive and a techno-managerial round. US candidates get a HireVue video first, then a manager call and a Superday.
