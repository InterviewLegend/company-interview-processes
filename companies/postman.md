# Postman software engineer interview process

Postman runs three to five rounds after a recruiter call, and can close within a week or drag past a month. A hiring-manager deep dive often cuts people. The rest is machine coding, an hour shipping REST endpoints in your own IDE, plus system design, often a product bar raiser and a culture-fit close.
