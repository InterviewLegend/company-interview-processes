# Palantir software engineer interview process

Palantir's interview process usually runs five to six stages over three to four weeks, from recruiter call to a hiring-manager final. In between sit a 60 to 90 minute HackerRank test, a live CodePair screen and a Superday of three rounds picked from four. Decomposition is near-universal there, and it decides most loops.

|---|---|
| Stages | 6 stages |
| Timeline | ~3–4 weeks |
| Online assessment | HackerRank |
