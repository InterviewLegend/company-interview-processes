# CrowdStrike software engineer interview process

A CrowdStrike engineer loop usually runs three to five rounds over several weeks. Expect a recruiter, live coding, one or two design rounds and a hiring manager probing a past project. Interns and new grads first sit a HackerRank test of about two hours. Experienced hires are judged mostly on design and the project talk.
