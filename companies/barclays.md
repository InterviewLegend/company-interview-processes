# Barclays software engineer interview process

Barclays' tech loop usually runs three to four weeks: an online assessment of about two hours, a CS-fundamentals technical round and a RISES values round. Hires at AVP and above add a finance-flavoured design round. People who prep only LeetCode-style algorithms tend to stumble on the fundamentals.
