# Spotify software engineer interview process

Spotify's process has five stages and takes two to five weeks when it moves fast. A recruiter call and a CoderPad screen lead to four or five onsite rounds: two coding, design on Mural, values, and a case study where you debug a stalled live system. That last round counts most.

| Stages | 5 stages |
| Timeline | ~2–5 weeks |
