# Cisco software engineer interview process

Cisco's software loop usually runs four to eight weeks: recruiter screen, often a HackerRank test, phone screen, and an onsite of three to five rounds. The test mixes in networking and OS multiple choice. The code bar is medium, and LeetCode-only prep tends to stall on those basics. Your business unit sets the rest.
