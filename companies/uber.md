# Uber software engineer interview process

Uber's loop usually runs four to six weeks: recruiter call, a CodeSignal test or phone screen, then four to six onsite rounds in one day. The test is four problems in roughly 70 minutes. Interviewers settle it in a debrief, where a Bar Raiser's doubt tends to down-level rather than reject.

|---|---|
| Stages | 4 stages |
| Timeline | ~4–6 weeks |
| Online assessment | CodeSignal |
