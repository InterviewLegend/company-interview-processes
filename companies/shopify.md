# Shopify software engineer interview process

Shopify's loop runs five to six remote rounds, and recruiter call to offer commonly takes three to five weeks. After a recruiter screen come the Life Story, a CoderPad screen, a 75 to 90 minute pairing build and a deep dive. Senior loops add system design, and a weak Life Story can end things early.

|---|---|
| Stages | ~6 stages |
| Timeline | ~3–5 weeks |
