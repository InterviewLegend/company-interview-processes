# Palo Alto Networks software engineer interview process

Palo Alto Networks usually takes a US candidate from application to offer in five or six weeks. A recruiter call comes first, then a timed HackerRank or Codility test of 60 to 90 minutes, a technical screen and three to four onsite rounds. Strong coders have been cut on thin OS and networking fundamentals.

| | |
|---|---|
| Stages | 3–4 onsite rounds |
| Timeline | ~5–6 weeks |
| Online assessment | HackerRank or Codility |
