# Databricks software engineer interview process

Databricks loops usually take three to eight weeks: a recruiter call, a CoderPad screen that runs your code, a hiring-manager chat, then five onsite rounds. Candidates most often call the concurrency hour the hardest. A committee weighs the feedback with your references, often a manager and two senior peers, and sets your level.
