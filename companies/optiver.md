# Optiver software engineer interview process

Optiver's engineer loop takes a few weeks: a two-hour HackerRank OA, an early behavioural chat, a tech interview, then a final day of three rounds. The OA cuts the most people, and scores near 90 percent have still been rejected. The famous 80-in-8 math sprint belongs to the trader loop.
