# X (Twitter) software engineer interview process

X's interview process runs through xAI and usually takes two to three weeks, from the first engineer call to an offer. A written Statement of Exceptional Work and a proctored, roughly 60-minute CodeSignal test precede two to four onsite rounds in Palo Alto. Candidates say the proctoring and clock break more people than the problems.
