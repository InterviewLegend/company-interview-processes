# PayPal software engineer interview process

A clean PayPal loop takes two to four weeks or longer: a recruiter call, usually a HackerRank OA, phone screens, then a virtual onsite. Karat runs many of the one or two phone screens. The Teams onsite holds four to six rounds, and the hiring manager's round weighs heavily in the final call.
