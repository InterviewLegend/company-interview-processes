# Nutanix software engineer interview process

Nutanix's MTS loop usually opens with a HackerRank test of two algorithm problems in 60 to 120 minutes, then four to six rounds. Expect two or three coding rounds, often a debugging round, one or two design rounds and a techno-managerial close. Rounds are often all elimination, and design alone sinks some strong coders.
