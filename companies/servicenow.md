# ServiceNow software engineer interview process

ServiceNow's R&D loop usually takes two to four weeks: a recruiter call, a HackerRank test, three or four live technical rounds, then a hiring-manager round. The test commonly runs 45 to 60 minutes, though not every pipeline has one. The medium-to-hard algorithm rounds settle the offer, and even the manager round may hand you code.
