# xAI software engineer interview process

xAI aims to finish its engineer loop in about a week, but two to four weeks is more realistic. An Exceptional Work Statement comes first, then a short engineer screen and a 45 to 60 minute coding screen. Three technical rounds follow. The team round, built on your roughly 20-minute hardest-problem talk, often decides it.
