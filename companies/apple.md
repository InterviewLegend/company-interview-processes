# Apple software engineer interview process

Apple's software engineer process takes about four to eight weeks, and each team runs its own loop. Most people get a 30-minute recruiter call, a hiring-manager chat and a technical phone screen. The onsite is often five or six rounds, and that manager makes the final call, with no Bar Raiser.
