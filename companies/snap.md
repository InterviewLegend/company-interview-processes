# Snap software engineer interview process

Snap's interview process usually runs three to six weeks: recruiter call, HackerRank coding screen, then a virtual onsite with four to six rounds. Kind, Smart and Creative are scored in nearly every round. You're matched to a team only after you accept, and that match can stall for weeks.
