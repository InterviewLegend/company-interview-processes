# Jane Street software engineer interview process

At Jane Street, the interview runs about five stages over a few weeks, split into software and trading tracks. Engineers take an hour-long coding screen, then an onsite of three to four roughly 70-minute coding rounds. Traders face a timed mental-math test first, and missing its bar ends the loop before the probability rounds.
