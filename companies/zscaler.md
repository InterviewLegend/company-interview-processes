# Zscaler software engineer interview process

Zscaler's India early-career loop opens with a pre-placement talk and a HackerRank test, then two or three technical rounds and a light HR chat. The test, 90 minutes to two hours for three or four medium-to-hard problems, cuts the most people. Senior loops are recruiter-led and typically run four or five rounds, adding system design.
